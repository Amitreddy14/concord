## Summary

A partner bank is processing outbound payout instructions on a delayed schedule. Affected
settlements remain in processing and will complete once the bank clears its queue. No funds are
at risk.

## What merchants see

A settlement past its expected date, still showing as processing, with no UTR recorded. Captures
and refunds are unaffected.

## Resolution

The bank has not committed to a completion time. No revised date should be given to merchants
until one is published here.
