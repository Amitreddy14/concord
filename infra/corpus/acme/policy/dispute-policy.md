## What a chargeback is

A chargeback is a buyer disputing a capture through their issuing bank. It is not a refund
request and it does not arrive through the merchant. The card network sets a deadline by which
evidence must be submitted, and that deadline is not extendable.

## Evidence

Contesting a dispute requires evidence appropriate to the reason code. For a
product-not-received dispute that is proof of delivery or of service provision. For a
fraudulent-transaction dispute it is evidence that the cardholder authorised the payment.

Evidence must be submitted before the stated deadline. A deadline that passes without a
submission is treated as the merchant accepting the chargeback, and the amount is debited.

## Accepting a dispute

A merchant may accept a chargeback rather than contest it. Acceptance is final and the disputed
amount is debited from the next settlement along with the network's dispute fee.

The decision to accept or contest belongs to the merchant and is never taken on their behalf.
