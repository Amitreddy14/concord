## Why payouts are held

Payouts are held while a merchant's verification is incomplete or under review. The hold applies
to settlements, not to the merchant's ability to accept payments; captures continue normally and
the funds accumulate until the hold is lifted.

## What to do next

When payouts are held for verification, re-upload the business registration certificate and a
current bank statement from the dashboard under Settings, Verification. A reviewer responds
within two working days of a fresh submission. Payouts resume automatically once verification
completes; no separate request is needed.

Documents must be legible, unexpired, and must match the registered business name exactly. A
mismatch between the uploaded document and the registered name is the most common cause of a
repeat review.

## What support can and cannot do

Support can confirm that a hold exists and which category it falls under. Support cannot lift a
hold, expedite a review, or disclose the specific finding that prompted it. These restrictions
are regulatory rather than discretionary.
