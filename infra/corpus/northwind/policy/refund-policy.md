## Eligibility window

A refund may be initiated within 90 days of the original capture. After that period the
transaction can no longer be reversed through the platform and the merchant must settle with
the customer directly.

The window runs from the capture date. Where a payment has several captures, each capture
carries its own window.

## Authorisation

A refund for a duplicate charge is processed without further review. Every other refund reason
is reviewed by the payments team before the funds leave, including a customer changing their
mind. This is a deliberate difference from other acquirers and is not a service delay.

## Credit timelines

Card refunds reach the cardholder's statement within 5 to 10 working days. SEPA credit transfers
are returned within 3 working days. The platform does not control the issuing bank's posting
schedule.

## Partial refunds

A capture may be refunded in part, and more than once, up to the unrefunded remainder of that
capture. The remainder shown against a capture is the authoritative figure.
