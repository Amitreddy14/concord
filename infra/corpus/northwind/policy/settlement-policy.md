## Settlement schedule

Settlements are initiated on the next working day after capture and are credited within two
further working days. Weekends and Irish public holidays are not working days.

## Holds

A settlement may be held pending a compliance review. The existence of a hold is visible to the
merchant in the dashboard; the reason for it is not disclosed by the support desk under any
circumstances, and no one on this desk can release one.

## Delays

Where a settlement has not arrived by its expected date, the expected date and current status in
the dashboard are the authoritative facts. The desk does not commit a new date for a settlement
that is delayed by an unresolved incident.
