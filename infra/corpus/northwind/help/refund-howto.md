## From the dashboard

Open the payment, select the capture, and choose Refund. Enter the amount, which may be the full
capture or part of it, and select a reason. The reason determines whether the refund is processed
immediately or queued for review.

## What the customer sees

The customer is not notified by the platform. Merchants who want the customer informed should
send their own confirmation, because the refund appears on the customer's statement without
explanation otherwise.

## If a refund is queued for review

A queued refund is held for up to 24 hours. If the payments team has not acted within that time
the request is abandoned and passed to a person, and the merchant is told so.
